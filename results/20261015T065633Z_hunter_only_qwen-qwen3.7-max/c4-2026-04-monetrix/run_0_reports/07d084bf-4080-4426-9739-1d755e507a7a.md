**Severity (estimated):** Medium  
**Confidence:** 0%  
**Affected:** totalBacking, totalBackingSigned, _readL1Backing

2. Perp positions on L1 suffer large unrealized losses (e.g., -12M USDC signed account value) due to adverse price movement before hedges can be repaired.

7. This could trigger false insolvency detection, block legitimate yield settlement (Gate 3: proposedYield ≤ distributableSurplus would see zero/negative surplus), or cause unnecessary peg-defense actions.
8. The correct behavior would be to floor only the signed perp component, not the entire aggregate, since EVM USDC and spot assets cannot go negative.
